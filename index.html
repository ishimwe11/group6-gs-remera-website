About the School

GS Remera TSS is located in Northern Province, Rulindo District, Cyinzuzi Sector. The school provides quality education and technical skills.

Departments

Nursery

This is the early childhood stage where young children begin school. They learn basic skills like counting, reading simple words, drawing, playing, and social interaction. The focus is on developing communication and motor skills in a friendly environment.

Primary

This level builds basic education skills. Pupils learn subjects such as Mathematics, English, Science, Social Studies, and Languages. It prepares learners with strong foundations for higher education.

O-Level

This is the intermediate level after primary school. Students study more advanced subjects in sciences, humanities, and languages. It prepares learners for national examinations and future careers or further studies.

Technical

This department focuses on practical and technical skills. In Software Development (SOD), students learn programming, web development, databases, and computer applications. It prepares learners for jobs in ICT and modern technology industries.
