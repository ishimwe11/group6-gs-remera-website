OTHER INFO

This department deepens students’ academic knowledge and skills. Learners specialize in different subject combinations depending on their interests and career goals. It also focuses on preparing students for national exams, higher education, and professional life. Leadership, discipline, and responsibility are also emphasized.

OBJECTIVES

  1. To provide advanced academic knowledge in different subjects
  2. To prepare students for national examinations and higher studies
  3. To develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills
  4. To guide students in choosing future careers
