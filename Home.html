General information


GS Remera TSS is a public school located in Rwanda, in the Northern Province, Rulindo District, Cyinzuzi Sector. The school consists of four main departments that provide quality education and skills development to students.
The GS REMERA/TSS is school institution has strong capacity to effectively support its students, providing a conducive learning environment with sufficient facilities, well-maintained equipment, and access to modern, advanced technology that enhances both practical and theoretical learning.

If you want an even more formal/academic version (for reports):
