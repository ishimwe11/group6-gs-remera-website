📘 School Rules & Regulations

1. Attendance

All students must attend school regularly and arrive on time.

2. Discipline

Students must respect teachers, staff, and fellow students at all times.

3. Uniform

Students must wear the official school uniform every school day.

4. Cleanliness

Keep the school environment clean and dispose of waste properly.

5. Academic Integrity

Cheating in exams or assignments is strictly prohibited.

6. Respect Property

Do not damage school property or belongings of others.

7. Mobile Phones

Use of mobile phones during class is not allowed unless permitted.

8. Safety

Follow all safety rules and report any dangerous situations.
