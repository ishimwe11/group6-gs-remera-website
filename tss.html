OTHER INFORMATION

This department is designed to equip learners with real-world skills in information technology and engineering-related disciplines, preparing them for both employment and self-employment after graduation. It plays an important role in supporting a country’s development by producing skilled technicians, developers, and innovators who can work in the modern digital economy.


In TSS, students are trained in areas such as software development, computer networking, database management, system design, and computer maintenance. Through projects and practical work, learners develop the ability to create, manage, and maintain technological systems used in schools, businesses, and organizations.

OBJECTIVES

  1. To teach ICT and technical skills such as programming, networking, and databases
  2. To prepare students for employment and entrepreneurship
  3. To develop practical and hands-on skills through projects
  4. To integrate technology into learning and real-life applications
